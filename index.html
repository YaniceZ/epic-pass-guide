How access actually works

Epic Pass is not one flat rulebook. Each resort sits in one of three tiers.

Unlimited

Vail Resorts' own mountains in North America. No day cap, no separate booking — just show up and ride.

no cap · direct scan

Pooled days

Resorts of the Canadian Rockies share one combined allowance across all six resorts.

7 days total · 50% off after

Partner access

Europe and Japan resorts on a fixed day allowance. Some require booking a designated partner hotel.

limited days · verify per resort
