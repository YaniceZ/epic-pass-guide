How access actually works

Three rulebooks, running at once. Which one applies decides whether a mountain is free or costs you a scarce day.

Unlimited

Vail Resorts' own mountains. On the full pass there is no day cap and no blackout — turn up and scan. On Epic Local the premium ones go dark on holidays.

no cap · scan and ride

Pooled days

Canadian Rockies partners draw from one shared allowance — six resorts, seven days between them. Spend them anywhere in the group.

7 days across 6 resorts · 50% off after

Partner access

Europe, Japan and Telluride, each with its own day count. Several must be used consecutively, and the My Epic phone pass does not open these gates.

3–7 days · physical card at the window

      Before you book

      01

      Carry the physical card abroad

      The My Epic phone pass will not open a lift gate at any partner resort. It only proves the pass is yours so the window can issue a ticket — bring the plastic.

      02

      Arlberg needs lodging first

      Its three days unlock only if you book directly with a participating property. Les 3 Vallées dropped that requirement for 2026–27, leaving Arlberg the last one holding it.

      03

      Watch opening day, not the calendar

      Target dates move with the snowfall, and an opening day often means one lift and a ribbon of white. Read the snow report in the fortnight before you drive.
